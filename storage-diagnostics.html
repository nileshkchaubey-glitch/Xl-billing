Important safety instructions

  • Avoid private/incognito windows for permanent billing; this tool cannot detect private mode.
  • Do not clear browser/site data or change profile, computer or origin without an independently verified private backup and a restore rehearsal.
  • Use one billing tab and one master device. Simultaneous editors or older app copies can overwrite changes.
  • A checksum or requested download does not prove external backup possession, correct accounting, or disk durability.
  • If evidence is corrupt, denied, partial or unexplained: stop creating entries in that profile, preserve the raw state, and seek recovery advice. Do not clear data.
